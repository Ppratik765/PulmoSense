Proposed architecture
End-to-end, entirely on the phone
STEP 1
Capture
16 kHz PCM audio during a 20 s guided breathing cycle
STEP 2
Preprocess
Sliding 5 s windows with 1 s overlap
STEP 3
Log-Mel
STFT, 128 Mel bands up to 8 kHz, dB scale
STEP 4
CNN
MobileNetV3-Large, ImageNet weights, top 50 layers fine-tuned
STEP 5
Diagnosis
8-class softmax, chunk outputs averaged
128 × 157 × 3
input tensor per chunk: four 5 s chunks per 20 s recording are classified and their softmax outputs averaged into one confidence score

Backup · Likely questions
Honest answers on limitations
Noisy rooms and phone mics?
Not tested yet. ICBHI is clinical-grade audio; an informal three-phone check showed visible spectrogram differences. Next: DEMAND noise contamination.
Is it memorising augmentations?
We used different augmentation in train and test and checked leakage, but it did not fully disappear. An independent dataset is the real test.
Children or other populations?
ICBHI is mostly adults. Paediatric acoustics differ (smaller airways, faster rate), so generalisation is unknown.
Is it clinically validated?
No. It is a screening aid. It needs prospective trials with IRB approval, comparison with pulmonary function tests and regulatory review.
